Field notes
  • HQ positions are candidates. Each team has one static gun at a time, at HQ or built.
  • Static guns can be built anywhere in your first two sectors. Uneven ground or a blocked barrel stops a spot.
  • The ring around your gun is radial range only, 100 to 1,600 m. It does not show how far the gun can turn, what blocks the barrel, or where it is safe to fire.
  • The gunner seat turns only so far. For wider angles, swing the gun from the loader seat.
  • Flight time is an estimate of about 24 s, not measured for the current update. Time a few shots and set your own.
  • In Offensive the attackers have no artillery squad, and Skirmish has none at all.
  • Height differences, shell spread and blast radius are not modelled.
