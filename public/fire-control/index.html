Field notes
  • Static guns can be built anywhere in your first two sectors, not only at HQ. Uneven ground or cover can still block a spot.
  • Each team has one static gun at a time, at HQ or built.
  • The enemy build zone is where their gun can be. An HQ position is only a first guess.
  • In Offensive the attackers have no artillery squad, and Skirmish has none at all.
  • Solutions are estimates. Height differences and self-propelled artillery are not modelled.
